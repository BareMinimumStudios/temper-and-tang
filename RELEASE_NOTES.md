# Temper & Tang 0.1.2 — first workshop prototype

A physical finishing anvil with a wooden base, copper fittings and an animated smithing hammer. Click the working hammer or press Space, follow the progress caption, then take the finished weapon. Minecraft metalworking sounds accompany accepted strikes and completion. A compact inventory tray and material tooltips explain the workflow.

Includes tin, bronze, four bounded finishes, Simply Swords/Simply More support, optional native Overgeared forging and hammer compatibility, and additive common ingredient tags. The bronze sword uses Overgeared forging when that integration is enabled. Pack-authored forging and quenching rules remain authoritative.

Both loader servers passed standalone and integration scenarios. The packaged NeoForge build passed background client checks for mouse/keyboard work, mouse output picking, costs, preservation, native quality, save/reload, cancellation and repeat-finish restrictions. See docs/VERIFICATION.md for scope and limitations.

This is a prototype. Further heating/shaping simulation, additional material profiles and live multiplayer playtesting remain future work. Several material icons and ore textures remain placeholders.

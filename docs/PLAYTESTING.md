# Playtesting 0.7.1

Use a backed-up test profile and world. Install the matching loader build and the libraries in [Installation](INSTALLATION.md). Keep your existing configuration; the numbers in the guides describe the defaults, which a pack may override.

## Try a complete piece

1. Make your starter bronze, hammer, tongs and shaping anvil. Confirm the recipes are visible in your recipe viewer.
2. Choose a sword pattern before heating its metal. Shape it with an empty cursor, take the actual unquenched workpiece with tongs, then quench and finish it.
3. Repeat with one armor piece. Check its ingredients, extra strikes, protection and durability. Try Tempered or Lightened and read the Shift tooltip.
4. If installed, forge a normal Simply Swords weapon and an Epic Knights armor piece. Scribe the required Epic Knights blueprint and confirm it remains after forging. Unique and legendary weapons are not part of the normal route.
5. Upgrade a steel piece to diamond, then netherite, with the required smithing templates. Check its name, enchantments, wear, craftsmanship and finish afterward.

## Check the feel

Compare a rough piece with a better result from the same material. Poor work should still be usable; high quality should reward practice. Check whether the rhythm is readable, harder materials feel harder, the hammer meets the metal, and station slots are easy to reach. Mention the material and station if anything looks misplaced.

## Check persistence and shared stations

Save and reload with a finished item and an unfinished workpiece. Confirm their details remain. In a separate multiplayer test, try two people using the workshop, leaving a station mid-process and reconnecting. Check that inputs, outputs and ownership behave normally and that no item appears twice.

For a bug report, include the loader, mod version, relevant config changes, exact item/pattern, station stage and steps to repeat it. Add the latest log or crash report if it failed to load. A screenshot helps with model or slot problems.

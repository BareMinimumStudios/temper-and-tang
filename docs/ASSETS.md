# Workshop artwork

The owner-supplied Blossom Studios Platinum Set v1.02 is the source of tin ore, deepslate ore, raw metal, blocks, ingot and nugget art and the bronze/steel tool and armor families. Source pixel silhouettes, rock, timber, leather and armor layout are preserved; metal ramps distinguish neutral-silver tin, warm bronze and darker cool steel. Tin equipment is not registered.

Run `scripts/adapt_platinum_textures.py` with the original ZIP and Pillow available to reproduce those adaptations. The source archive remains outside the repository. Flux, finishing materials, bronze blend, blueprints, upgrade template and unfinished-workpiece icons have dedicated transparent 16×16 sprites. The supplied Workshop Six tool and furniture models remain in use.

References: [Overgeared](https://modrinth.com/mod/overgeared) and [Bronze](https://modrinth.com/mod/bronze) informed the metal color distinction. Their textures are not bundled. Ordinary optional weapon art resolves from the installed weapon mod; no fallback dagger/spear/warhammer textures are shipped.

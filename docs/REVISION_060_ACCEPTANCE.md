## Evidence provenance and limits

Runtime suites were run throughout the revision, with their build checksums retained. The final NeoForge candidate is byte-for-byte identical to the gameplay/configuration candidate. Fabric's final change after those checks is its optional JEI metadata entrypoint; archive-entry comparison confirms gameplay classes/resources are unchanged. The updated Fabric JAR was then exercised with JEI alone, both viewers and a viewer-free dedicated server. `candidate-network-class-equivalence.json` verifies all 14 NeoForge / 15 Fabric station, custody, timing and loader-entrypoint classes match the actual two-client custody-test JARs.

The full-pack copy uses a local startup thread limit to avoid an existing MidnightLib initialization race. Its existing Small Ships shutdown-config exception is not a Temper & Tang fix and was not changed in New Test. Native external models and custom combat/projectile implementations remain owned by their mods. Common tags permit compatible ingredients; they do not replace Almost Unified's canonical-output policy. Old Exceptional workmanship is read as Masterwork and refreshed when processed, not by an unsolicited bulk inventory migration.

All requested revision acceptance gates above are satisfied. Longer balance playtesting and additional third-party integrations can continue as future revisions; they are not being represented as untested required features in this revision.

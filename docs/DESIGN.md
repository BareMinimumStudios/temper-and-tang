# Design

Temper & Tang is a standalone medieval smithing mod. Player skill, material choice and deliberate finishing matter in its workshop. Libraries provide configuration and world-space interaction; another smithing mod is never required.

The owned workflow is refined stock → charcoal heating → patterned shaping → quenching → optional finishing. Smithing Tongs safely carry hot stock in either hand. Contact burns and tool wear are bounded per player, and cooling cannot turn an unfinished workpiece into a usable weapon.

Current layers are equipment pattern, material, accuracy, server-random craftsmanship, smithing mastery, a bounded equipment trait and precision finishing. Names, damage, enchantments and foreign components must survive processing. Quality grading, maker records and reusable blueprints belong to this standalone system. Mastery nudges the odds without replacing player skill. Randomness must never let consistently poor work outperform careful work on average. Grinding and further material profiles remain future work; no external quality component defines this progression.

Shipped milestones include server-authoritative Diegesis workstations, original workstation/control geometry, tin and bronze, survival recipes, deterministic ghost patterns, readable hammer timing, owned handling tools and bounded finishes. All completion and consumption runs on the server.

Small recorded material profiles and a single equipment-appropriate trait now supplement quality. Further original weapon variants and bespoke ability integrations remain future work. Existing third-party metal patterns are supported through common stock tags and recipe routing. Keep actions short and accessible. Prefer deliberate selectable patterns over weapon roulette. Recipes should remain useful with medieval wind, water and coal workshops rather than requiring advanced Create machinery.

# Workshop design

Temper & Tang is a standalone smithing mod. The work follows a clear path: mine and refine metal, make an alloy when needed, heat it, shape a pattern, quench the workpiece and choose a finish.

Accuracy has the greatest influence on craftsmanship. Mastery helps and luck adds variation. There are five grades. Poor work remains usable; the highest grade should take care and practice. Material and a single bounded trait add smaller differences. Enchantments remain an extra enhancement.

Keep the work visible on the station. The hammer meets the metal, sparks and sound follow the impact, the bellows feed the coals, and the tongs lower a real workpiece into water. Guidance stays short; H and Shift reveal the rest. The pattern list shows ingredients, not internal recipe identifiers.

Use common tags and the owning equipment's recipes and statistics. Preserve names, enchantments, wear and foreign components. Processing replaces workshop bonuses rather than stacking them. Costs, output ownership and timing are checked by the server. A cancelled or interrupted session must not duplicate an item or charge twice.

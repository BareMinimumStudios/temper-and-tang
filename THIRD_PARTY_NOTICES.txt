Vanilla Minecraft item texture references resolve from the user's game installation.

The workshop impact sounds are original deterministic modal synthesis.
Their generator is provided in scripts/generate_workshop_audio.py.
Supplementaries was inspected as a bellows animation reference; none of its
geometry, textures, sounds or code are included.
